Batches

A batch is the set of frames you label together in one round: pre-label it, send it to Roboflow, bring the corrections back. Only frames that aren't in another batch are picked, so no frame is labelled twice. In Roboflow each frame lands in train, valid or test by its video's split; splits with no videos in the batch are left out.
